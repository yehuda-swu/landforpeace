## Interpretation note

These sources are included as a starting point for historical inquiry. The choice and categorisation of events reflect teaching decisions rather than a comprehensive historical dataset. The graphic avoids claiming that every peace initiative was rejected, that all events were comparable in territorial scale, or that every diplomatic negotiation included a concrete land offer.

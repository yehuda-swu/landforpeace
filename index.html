Teacher guide · approximately 5 minutes

1. Predict · 30 seconds

Ask students whether territorial compromise came only from governments on the left. Reveal all three colours.

2. Investigate · 2 minutes

Ask each pair to select one completed withdrawal and one proposal that did not become an agreement. What is the difference?

3. Compare · 1 minute

Use Equal size to remove the editorial scale. Ask whether a percentage of Hebron can be compared with a percentage of Sinai.

4. Discuss · 90 seconds

What does this timeline demonstrate about Israeli policy? Why might two sides disagree even when major territorial concessions are discussed?

Accuracy note: This is a deliberately selected timeline, not a complete count of peace offers. The Oslo agreements appear in several stages. Government labels simplify coalition politics, particularly for Ariel Sharon and Ehud Olmert. Where a proposal was confidential or disputed, the graphic says so. The text distinguishes land transfers, negotiated agreements, conditional proposals and diplomacy.

Teaching conclusion: Territorial compromise has been explored under different Israeli governments, but the history is more complicated than a series of ever larger offers being rejected. Students should examine the actual terms, the political circumstances and the responses of both parties.

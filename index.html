Educator guide

A short classroom activity for students aged 16 to 17. The outcome starts hidden every time students select a new moment.

01 / Predict · 30 seconds

Ask which political tradition students expect to be more likely to propose territorial compromise. Then show all three colours.

02 / Discuss · 2 minutes

Choose a moment. Read what Israel proposed or did. Ask students the question before clicking Reveal outcome.

03 / Compare · 1 minute

Compare a completed withdrawal with a proposal or talks that did not produce an agreement. Why might outcomes differ?

04 / Reflect · 90 seconds

Switch to equal circles. Ask how an illustrative graphic can shape our understanding of history. Consider differing security, diplomatic and political priorities.

Historical care: The 13 entries are a selected sample, not a complete census of peace proposals. Several Oslo entries belong to the same negotiation process. The categories describe the leading governing parties at the time, not every coalition partner. Dates and sources are listed under Sources.

Teaching conclusion: Governments across the political spectrum have explored territorial compromise. Some initiatives led to treaties or withdrawals. Others did not. Students should distinguish proposals from completed agreements and explore why negotiations succeed or fail.
